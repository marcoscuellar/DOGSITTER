02Complete user journeys

Pet owner

  1. Welcome & onboarding

    Register household, contact and emergency information; complete applicable care agreements.

  2. Add a dog

    Create Luna’s profile with photo, breed, age, personality, preferences, medication and feeding instructions. Add multiple dogs.

  3. Home dashboard

    See upcoming bookings, next visit, pets and messages.

  4. Book a service

    Choose walking, Day Club or Hotel Stay, dates and available arrival/pickup windows.

  5. Booking request

    Submit request; Leon reviews it. Payment and confirmation follow booking rules.

  6. Prepare for care

    Pack the bag, complete checklist, add medication, feeding and bedtime instructions and reference photos.

  7. During the visit

    Receive walk updates, photos, care notes, messages and notifications.

  8. Visit completed

    Access daily recaps, previous walks, overnight stays and booking history.

Leon / Service provider

  1. Business setup

    Configure services, availability, drop-off windows and business settings.

  2. Booking request

    Review the dog profile and approve or decline the request.

  3. Today’s dashboard

    See dogs in care, upcoming visits and responsibilities.

  4. Visit schedule

    Open separate schedules for walks, arrivals, pickups and overnight stays.

  5. Open dog card

    Review instructions; Start Walk and End Walk inside the individual dog card. No GPS tracking.

  6. Care checklist

    Record meals, walks and medication; add photos, notes or concerns.

  7. Communicate

    Send owner updates and messages; keep private notes separate.

  8. Complete visit

    Close out care, preserve history and provide an owner summary.
